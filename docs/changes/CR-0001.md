# CR-0001 — After-sales routes to published branch-office emails

## 1. Why
Jyotech's website contact page publishes branch-office email addresses, so the
original assumption behind after-sales routing no longer holds. Rather than send
every after-sales enquiry to sales@ with the region only in the subject line
(the interim workaround in assumption A-1), the agent should route directly to
the published branch-office email for the customer's region, copying sales@.
This gets service/spares/AMC enquiries to the people who can act on them fastest
and directly supports goal G-3 (route after-sales correctly).

## 2. PRD change
Modify only — no requirement id added or withdrawn. Version bump MINOR: PRD 1.0 → 1.1.

| Requirement | Change | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRD-F-006 | Modified | "…and route to the regional office for the customer's state, copying sales@. It shall not give troubleshooting advice." | "…and route the enquiry to the published branch-office email for the customer's region (copying sales@); where no branch-office email is published it shall fall back to sales@ with the region in the subject. It shall not give troubleshooting advice." |
| A-1 (assumption) | Updated | "Regional office emails are not printed publicly; after-sales routes to sales@ with region in the subject until Jyotech supplies them." | "Branch-office emails are published on the website contact page; after-sales routes to the branch-office email for the customer's region (copying sales@), falling back to sales@ with region in the subject only where no office email is published." |

What changed: the routing target for after-sales handoff moves from "sales@ with
region in subject" (interim) to the published branch-office email for the region
(To:), with sales@ as a copy and the old behaviour retained only as a fallback
when no office email exists. The collect-slots and no-troubleshooting clauses of
PRD-F-006 are unchanged.
